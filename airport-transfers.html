Private airport transfers

Stress-free from the moment you land

Long flights are exhausting enough. The last thing you need is to navigate an unfamiliar airport, queue for a cab, and argue with an app about your address. SSJ Exclusive handles all of that before you've even collected your luggage.

Your host will be waiting in arrivals — name board in hand — ready to take your bags and walk you to a clean, comfortable vehicle. From there, it's a smooth ride straight to your hotel, villa, or wherever you're calling home for the trip.

Available for solo travellers, couples, families, and corporate guests. Return transfers can be booked at the same time — one less thing to think about.

What's included
  • Meet & greet at arrivals
  • Flight tracking & delay monitoring
  • Clean, comfortable private vehicle
  • Assistance with all luggage
  • Bottled water on request
  • Local tips & recommendations en route
  • Return transfer booking available
