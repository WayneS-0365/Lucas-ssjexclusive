Private Cape Town tours

See the city like a local.
Not like a tourist.

Cape Town has the landmarks — Table Mountain, the V&A Waterfront, Bo-Kaap — and they're genuinely spectacular. But the city also has a second layer that most visitors never reach: the viewpoints locals actually use, the beaches without the crowds, the neighbourhoods worth lingering in.

Your SSJ host knows both layers. The tour is yours to shape — tell us what interests you, and we'll build a day around it. Or just say "surprise me" and trust the local.

Pickup from your hotel, villa, or Airbnb. Drop-off wherever you need. Full-day or half-day, your call.

City highlights

The full Cape Town experience

Table Mountain, Bo-Kaap, Signal Hill, the V&A Waterfront, Green Point — the iconic stops, done properly. Time at each one, not a drive-by.

Cape Peninsula

Chapman's Peak to Cape Point

The full peninsula circuit — dramatic coastal cliffs, the penguins at Boulders Beach, Cape Point, and the drive back along the Atlantic. A full day, brilliantly spent.
