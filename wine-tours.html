Private winelands tours

There's a reason visitors to the Western Cape often say the winelands were the highlight of their trip — not just the wine, but the mountain scenery, the historic estates, the long lunches that somehow become the afternoon.

With SSJ Exclusive, you get a private host and vehicle for the day. No shared minibuses. No rushed stops. No one else's itinerary. Just you, your group, and three of South Africa's most celebrated wine regions within comfortable reach.

Where we go

Three world-class regions.
One unforgettable day.

Stellenbosch

South Africa's most celebrated wine capital

Oak-lined streets, historic Cape Dutch estates, and bold reds that punch well above their weight internationally. A full day here barely scratches the surface — but what a scratch it is.

Franschhoek

French elegance, mountain drama, extraordinary food

Tucked into a mountain valley, Franschhoek offers world-class MCC, Michelin-calibre restaurants, and scenery that stops you mid-sentence. Plan a long lunch — you'll want one.

Constantia

Cape Town's closest wine valley — and oldest

Just 20 minutes from the city, Constantia's estates produce some of South Africa's finest whites. Perfect for a half-day add-on or a relaxed full morning before heading back.
