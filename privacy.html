Your information

Privacy policy.

How we collect, use and protect your personal information when you contact or book with us.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how SSJ Exclusive Transfers & Tours handles your personal information, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). It also covers visitors from outside South Africa.

1. Who we are

The responsible party is SSJ Exclusive, trading as SSJ Exclusive Transfers & Tours, South Africa.

Our Information Officer is Lucas Finnish. Contact: info@ssjexclusive.com.

2. What we collect

When you send a booking request or contact us, you may give us:

  • your name and email address;
  • the service you want, your preferred date and your group size;
  • any details you add in the message box, such as a flight number, hotel name or tour preferences;
  • your WhatsApp number and the messages you send us, if you contact us on WhatsApp.

We only need the number of children in your group, not their names or other personal details. Please don't include sensitive information such as health details or identity numbers in your message.

3. Why we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and prepare or confirm your booking;
  • to arrange your transfer or tour, for example meeting you at the airport;
  • to keep business records and meet legal obligations.

We use your information on the basis that you have asked us to, that it is needed to take steps towards a booking, or that you have agreed to it by sending your request. Giving us your name and email is needed for us to respond. Everything else is optional, but may mean we can't arrange your service properly. We don't sell your information and we don't use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run this website and our communications. They handle your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Formspree, which processes the booking form;
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website and routes our email;
  • Google (Gmail), where we receive and answer email;
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you message us there.

Some of these providers store or process information outside South Africa, including in the United States and Europe. We only use providers that apply appropriate data-protection safeguards. Where a driver or partner needs your details to complete your booking, we share only what they need.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for 24 months after your last enquiry or booking, or longer if the law requires it. After that we delete or anonymise it.

6. How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information, including secure connections (HTTPS), restricted access to our email and accounts, and two-factor authentication. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as the law requires.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary technical cookies to keep the site secure. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, where we no longer need it;
  • stop using it, or withdraw your consent.

Email info@ssjexclusive.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have similar rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR, and you can use the same contact.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
