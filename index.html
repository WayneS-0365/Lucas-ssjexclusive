Chapman's Peak Drive

The road that makes people pull over

Nine kilometres carved into a cliff face. Atlantic on one side, mountain on the other. It's the kind of drive you don't forget — and yes, we always stop for photos.

Franschhoek Valley

Where the mountains meet the vineyards

French Huguenot history, mountain-framed estates, and some of the finest food in South Africa. Bring an appetite — for everything.

Cape Point

The edge of the continent

Wild coastline, dramatic cliffs, and brazen baboons who have absolutely no concept of personal space. A proper adventure at the southern tip of Africa.
